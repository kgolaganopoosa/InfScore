Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

At InfScore, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy informs you about how we handle your information when you use our web application.

1. Local Client-Side Data Processing

InfScore is designed as a privacy-first, client-side web application. All personal details, handles, and follower counts entered into the calculator are processed directly within your browser window. No social media handles or personal metrics are transmitted or stored on external database servers.

2. PDF Document Generation

When generating an InfScore PDF certificate, the document is compiled locally on your browser using client-side JavaScript rendering engines (`jsPDF` and `html2canvas`). The generated PDF is downloaded directly to your device.

3. Local Storage

We do not store persistent tracking cookies. Temporary browser memory is utilized strictly for instant calculations while the application tab is active.
